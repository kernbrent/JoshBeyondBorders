The heart behind the journey

Bringing people together, beyond borders.

Joshua has been making music seriously since 2005, performing and leading worship in Boston, Dallas, Paris, England, and beyond. Through every setting, he has seen how music can create room for people to feel known, connected, and hopeful.

“My hope is to bring people together, create harmony, and make people feel seen.”

October 9–November 9, 2026

The journey

  1. Oct 9

    Depart for England

    Begin a month centered on music, ministry, cultural learning, and relationships.

  2. Oct 9–18

    London · Manchester · Liverpool

    Connect with churches and musicians, explore worship opportunities, attend cultural events, and deepen creative perspective.

  3. Oct 18–Nov 9

    Cape Town · Durban · Johannesburg

    Serve alongside local churches and trusted partners, especially in township communities and among vulnerable people groups.

  4. Nov 9

    Return home

    Bring home lessons, relationships, stories, and renewed creative direction.
